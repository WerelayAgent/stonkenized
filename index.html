On the ballot

72 tokenized equities trade on Robinhood Chain, but a winner nobody can buy without ruinous slippage helps no one. So the ballot is measured the honest way: every few hours the app quotes a real round-sized purchase through the same router it buys with, and lists the price impact. Anything that can't fill cleanly is off the ballot.

What happens at round close

1 · Fees arrive

STONKENIZED's trading tax is paid into the fee wallet on-chain.

2 · The winner is bought

The pot is swapped into the winning tokenized stock through an aggregator.

3 · Holders are paid

A snapshot is taken at close, and the stock is pushed out pro-rata to every eligible holder in batched transfers. No claiming, no gas for you.
